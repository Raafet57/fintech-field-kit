# Papers 2025 — Fintech & Digital Money Library

A neatly sorted stash of 2024–2025 papers on stablecoins, CBDCs, tokenization, real-time payments, FX, core banking, and compliance. Built for curious bankers, regulators, builders, and consultants who want to look smart on Zoom with minimal effort.

## What’s inside
- Stablecoins & CBDC: policy, design, and regulatory takes (ECB, IMF, independent voices).
- Tokenization & blockchain: digital asset rails, tokenized funds, and governance myths.
- Payments & liquidity: instant settlement, RT-SCL, SEPA Instant, BIS fast payments.
- FX & currency strategy: dollar dependence, multi-currency treasuries, FXaaS.
- Core banking & lending: modernization playbooks, lending suite case studies, T24 programs.
- Compliance & standards: ISO 20022 lessons, crypto-asset frameworks, financial crime risk.
- Market research: fintech landscapes (Egypt, WEF jobs), big tech credit, digital finance frontiers.
- AI & digital transformation: productivity and supervisory toolkits.

## How to use this (and look clever doing it)
1) Browse by topic → institution to grab the most relevant PDF fast.  
2) Cherry-pick a few stats/charts for your next client deck.  
3) Compare policy stances (BIS/IMF/ECB) vs vendor pitches for a balanced view.  
4) Post a hot take on LinkedIn; link back here for the full buffet.
